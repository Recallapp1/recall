## Points for the lawyer

Everything in `docs/legal-drafts/LAWYER-BRIEF.md` (19 questions), plus from this round:

1. Rows 8 and 9: is erasing unlinked calls from a shared number, and exporting every call from a number to its only patient, the right reading of KVKK m.7 and m.11?
2. Row 10: may appointments booked for a relative under another patient's record be moved to the relative's own record (data rewrite), or must they stay?
3. Row 11: is a generic appointment SMS to a shared family phone acceptable, and should the notice say so?
4. Row 12: periods for call summaries, demo requests and audit events.
5. Row 16: what must the assistant offer when a caller objects to recording?
6. Correction (row 15): is staff correction with an audit entry naming the fields enough, or must the patient's request be recorded too?
